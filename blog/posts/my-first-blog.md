I have always thought about what the best way would be to learn something new. The process is different for different people, of course. I have tried a lot of various things. I have attempted to have schedules, do courses, free style learning things as and when I feel like it. There's always been a part of me that wanted to write a blog and document things. The hope is that this helps me stay focused and be more structured about what I learn and maybe help some people along the way.

This isn't meant to be a professional blog at all. It will be a dump of my current thoughts and ideas. There is a non-zero chance that the information I post is wrong. Any corrections are welcome. I just want to document my learning process and hopefully become better in the process.

Hopefully this becomes a tradition and we are all a bit smarter and happier a couple of blog posts later.
